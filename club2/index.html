Fair questions

Do I need to join to use the apps?

No. myStereo and myShelf stay free for everyone. Membership is for people who want the card, the letters and the gatherings.

Will it renew on its own?

Card only never renews. Member and Backstage renew through Substack, and you can cancel there anytime.

Do I have to come to things?

Never. Some members come to every gathering, some just like the letters and the card. Both count.

Where does the money go?

Printing cards, stickers, postage, keeping the sites running, and the occasional record.

Where are gatherings?

Around the Twin Cities, at places on the list. Dates go to members first.
