Alpha tester · Quick Start

Welcome to Flipside.

You’re one of the first people making mixtapes on Flipside. Five minutes gets you from nothing to a tape in a friend’s hands. Then tell BC what happened.

Get going

  1. Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: open ownyourmedia.club/flipside/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app. On a computer, just open the link.

  2. Sign in to Plex

    On the deck, tap Library, then Settings › Music Sources › Plex. No Plex handy? Choose Demo Library and try everything with sample songs.

  3. Make a tape

    Press REC. Name it, pick a C60 or C90, and tap songs to record them. Songs too long for the room left are crossed out. When a side is full, it’s full.

  4. Play it

    STOP/EJECT opens the door, tap your tape, press PLAY. Hold REW or FF to wind. FLIP turns it over.

  5. Send it

    Open the tape and choose Send Mixtape. The list shows people you share your Plex server with. They hear it from your music, so share your library with them in Plex first.

Try these too

  • A song you don’t own yet. On a tape’s menu, choose + A Song You Don’t Own. Buy it, add it to Plex, and watch it fill in.
  • Deck colors. Settings › Deck Color. Sport Yellow changes both sides of the player.
  • On a computer. Switch to Desktop Mode for a big deck and a recording panel.
  • Dub to Phone. Save a tape to your phone and play it with no signal.

Tell BC

On the library side, choose Alpha › Send Feedback. Leave Include device details checked; it tells BC which phone and settings you had. Alpha › What to Test lists what BC wants to hear about most.

Broken, confusing, too small, or just right: all of it helps. No mail app? Write to bc@runawayanchor.com.

Good to know

  • It’s an alpha. Things will break. Your tapes are saved to your Plex server, so they’re safe.
  • Phones only start sound after a tap. If the first PLAY is silent, press it again.
  • Tape missing on another device? Mixtapes › Sync with Plex.
  • Flipside updates itself. If the icon looks old, remove it and add it again.

More help: the Flipside User’s Guide and its troubleshooting chapter.
