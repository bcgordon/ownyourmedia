Welcome to Flipside.

You’re one of the first people making mixtapes on Flipside. Five minutes gets you from nothing to a tape in a friend’s hands. Then tell BC what happened.

Get going

  1. Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: open ownyourmedia.club/flipside/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app. On a computer, just open the link.

  2. Sign in to Plex

    On the deck, tap Menu, then Settings › Your Music › Plex. No Plex handy? Choose Demo Library and try everything with sample songs.

  3. Make a tape on your bench

    Press REC, then + New Tape. Name it, pick a C60 or C90, and add songs to Side A and Side B. When a side is full, it’s full. Want help? Plan it first with the Coach.

  4. Send it to the rack

    New tapes stay on your bench until they’re done. Open the Tape tab, check the list and press Send to the Rack.

  5. Play it

    STOP/EJECT opens your tape rack. Tap a tape to lift it out, press LOAD TAPE, then PLAY. Hold REW or FF to wind. FLIP turns it over; ⏮ RESTART goes back to the start of the side.

  6. Send it to a friend

    Press SEND MIXTAPE on the deck. They hear it from your music, so share your Plex library with them first.

Try these too

  • File it in a drawer. In the rack, lift a tape and press ASSIGN to put it in Morning, Road Trip or a drawer of your own. The color band on a tape shows its drawer group.
  • Rework a tape. Lift it in the rack and press REWORK. It goes back to your bench, drawers and notes and all, until you send it back.
  • Say why. Tap a song in the deck’s SONGS list and write a line about why it’s on the tape. It travels with the tape.
  • Put it on the TV. Screen Mirror your phone, then Menu › TV Mode. The tape, the song and your note, made for the couch.
  • Plan one first. + New Tape › Need help planning? Fill a crate, pick a story arc, put the keepers in order and let the Mixtape Coach help. Then Record It.
  • Dress up the label. The bench’s Label tab: one tap for a Theme, or pick each part yourself, from the Design to the Shell. ✦ Surprise Me if you can’t decide.
  • On every device. Tapes, drawers, plans and the Wishlist follow you through Plex. Works in progress show in Plexamp with a ✎.
  • Analog Mode. Settings › On Your Phone › Analog Mode puts every tape on your phone so Flipside works with no signal. Dub on Wi-Fi.

Tell BC

Press ✎ NOTE on the deck any time something breaks, gives you an idea or makes you smile. Write a line and press SEND NOW, or save it and later use Menu › Alpha › Notepad › Send All to BC. Alpha › What to Test lists what BC most wants to hear about.

Broken, confusing, too small, or just right: all of it helps. No mail app? Write to bc@runawayanchor.com.

Good to know

  • It’s an alpha. Things will break. Your tapes are saved to your Plex server, so they’re safe.
  • Phones only start sound after a tap. If the first PLAY is silent, press it again.
  • Listening with the screen locked? Close myStereo first, so the lock screen only has Flipside on it.
  • New tape not in the rack? It’s still on your bench: Tape tab › Send to the Rack.
  • Tape missing on another device? Sync with Plex, on your bench.
  • A friend can’t play your tape? Share your Plex music library with them, then send it again.
  • More help: Settings › Help & About, or ownyourmedia.club/flipside/app/guide.html
