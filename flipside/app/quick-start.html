Quick Start · Flipside 1.9

Get going

  1. Put it on your Home Screen.iPhone: open flipside.ownyourmedia.club in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.
  2. Bring your music.The welcome card asks. This Phone opens Files: pick a few songs you own. My Plex signs you in to your server.
  3. Make a tape.Press REC, write the label, pick C60 or C90, and add songs to Side A and Side B. When a side is full, it’s full.
  4. Finish it.On the Finish tab, press Send to the Rack. Your tape is lifted out of the rack: press LOAD TAPE, then PLAY.
  5. Send it.Press SEND, check the songs, then GET THE LINK. Your friend hears it one song at a time.

Try these too

Liner notes. On the Label tab, a few lines about the whole tape. They travel with it.

Why it’s here. In the deck’s SONGS list, tap a song and write why it’s on the tape. While it plays, ✎ NOTE shows on the readout.

Fill. A side with a minute or more left shows a Fill button with songs that fit.

Drawers. Lift a tape in the rack and press ASSIGN to put it in Morning, Road Trip or one of your own.

Real Tape. Got a tape deck with a line in? Lift a tape and press TAPE OUT to record it.

Plan one. From your second tape on, the bench adds the Crate, the Coach and songs you don’t own yet.

Tell BC

Press ✎ NOTE on the deck whenever something breaks, gives you an idea or makes you smile. SEND NOW sends it straight to BC. Menu › Alpha › What to Test lists what he most wants to hear about.

No mail app? bc@runawayanchor.com

Good to know

It’s an alpha. Things will break.

Phones only start sound after a tap. If the first PLAY is silent, press it again.

Use the Home Screen app, not a private Safari tab. Private tabs forget everything when you close them.

Save a backup now and then: Settings › Your Music › Back Up My Tapes. Always do it before removing Flipside from your Home Screen.

A song won’t add? If it’s in iCloud Drive, open it once in Files so it downloads, then try again.

The full User’s Guide is in Settings › Help & About.
