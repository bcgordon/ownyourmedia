A short guide

The Art of
the Mixtape.

For a long time, the way to tell someone how you felt was to sit by the stereo with a blank tape and a stack of records, and choose. Ninety minutes. Two sides. A label in your own handwriting. Here’s what that taught us.

§1 Ten rules of thumb

  1. Make it for someone

    A playlist is for anybody. A mixtape is for one person. Picture them pressing play, and pick for them, not for everybody.

  2. Limits are the point

    Forty-five minutes a side means you have to choose. Gather far too many songs, then cut until it hurts a little. The cutting is where the tape gets good.

  3. Open strong

    The first song says hello. In a few seconds it tells them what kind of tape this is and why they should keep listening.

  4. Tell a story

    Build, peak, breathe, land. Put the songs in an order that goes somewhere, the way a good album or a good night does.

  5. Mind the flip

    Side A’s last song is a cliffhanger. Side B’s first song is a fresh start. The pause in between is part of the music.

  6. Spread the voices

    Don’t put the same artist back to back. Mix old and new, loud and quiet, famous and obscure. Surprise them.

  7. Listen to the seams

    Play the last seconds of one song into the first seconds of the next. A good tape flows; a great one makes the jumps feel like they were meant to be.

  8. End on purpose

    The last song is what they’ll hum afterward. Make it the goodbye you mean.

  9. Write it by hand

    The label and the song list are part of the gift. Your handwriting says you made this. A note about why a song is there says even more.

  10. Give it away

    A mixtape isn’t finished until someone presses play.

§2 Plan it with the Coach

In Flipside, press REC, write the label and choose “Plan it with the Coach.”

The Crate

Grab every song that might fit, in any order, with no time limit. It’s the maybe pile. Choose later.

Story arcs

Pick a shape for the tape, or build your own beats. Each beat explains its job, so you know what kind of song goes there.

The Running Order

Move the keepers in from the crate and put them in order. Press and hold a song, then drag it. The Plan tab always shows whether it fits a C60 or a C90.

Hold a Spot

Know you need “something slow to cool down, about four minutes” but not which song? Hold the spot and fill it later.

Why It’s Here

A note on any song about why it made the tape. It’s for you now and for them later.

The Mixtape Coach

Gentle notes on the rules of thumb: an artist twice in a row, a long opener, the song right before the flip. Dismiss any note, or turn the Coach off once you know the craft.

§3 Every tape has a shape

Or build your own: name each beat for the feeling, and say what that part of the tape does.
