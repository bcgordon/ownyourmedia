A mixtape is a few songs picked for one person, in an order that means something. Flipside gives you a C60 or a C90, two sides, a label you write yourself and a cassette deck with real keys. Ninety minutes makes you choose. That’s the whole point.

§1 Make it. Play it. Send it.

Make it

Press REC, write the label and pick a C60 or a C90. Each side shows the time left, and a song too long for the room is crossed out. When a side is full, it’s full.

Play it

A cassette deck with real keys. REW and FF wind with the sound of the tape, and at the end of Side A you flip it. Tapes remember where you stopped.

Send it with Apple Music

Have an Apple Music subscription? Send a tape made from your own library to anyone else who has one. Flipside turns it into a link; they tap it and the tape is in their deck, Side A cued. Friends without Apple Music still hear a 30-second preview of each song.

No Apple Music?

Share it on your own network: anyone you’ve invited to your Plex server hears the tape from your library, every song just as you have it. Or use TAPE OUT and record it onto a real cassette you can hand them.

§2 A tape that looks like you made it

Write the label

Eighteen looks named for kinds of music, from Rock and Jazz to Chiptune. Or pick the paper, the pen, the ink and the shell yourself. Stuck? Surprise Me.

Liner notes

A few lines about the whole tape: who it’s for, what it’s about, when to play it. They go with the tape when you send it.

Why it’s here

A note on one song. It shows on the deck while that song plays.

Deck colors

Night Black, Metallic Blue, Pearl & Teal and more. Turn the player over and the menu side matches.

§3 Plan it with the Coach

A great mixtape is often planned before it’s made. Pick a story arc (Road Trip, For a Crush, Heartbreak, Bright Side, Late Side), fill the crate with maybes, and put the keepers in order. Hold a spot for “something slow, about four minutes.” The Mixtape Coach points out the rules of thumb, like an artist twice in a row or a long opener, and gets out of the way.

§4 Real Tape

Record your tape onto a real cassette. Lift a tape in the rack and press TAPE OUT. Flipside checks that each side fits the tape, with the quiet between songs counted, and walks you through a short list: phone wired to the deck’s line in, Do Not Disturb on, volume up, phone charging, blank tape at the start of Side A.

Set your levels with a tone, press record on the deck, and Flipside plays the side with four seconds of quiet between songs, so your deck can find every break. Every Flipside C90 fits a real C90.

Then hand it to someone. That’s the best part.

§5 And a few more things

Songs you don’t own yet

Put the perfect song on a tape before you have it. It holds its spot, with links to buy it from Bandcamp, Qobuz or the iTunes Store. Add the file and it fills itself in.

Turn it sideways

Widescreen Mode: the tape turning on one side, the album art and song in big letters on the other. Made for mirroring to a TV.

No signal

Songs on your phone play anywhere. Using Plex? Analog Mode saves your tapes to the phone for the plane or the cabin.

Inside myStereo too

Flipside is the Tapes button on myStereo. Same tapes, same deck.

§6 Get it on your phone

  1. Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: open flipside.ownyourmedia.club in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen. It gets its own cassette icon.

  2. Tell it where your music is

    This Phone picks songs from the Files app. My Plex brings your whole library. Just looking? Try the demo songs.

  3. Press REC

    Name it, pick C60 or C90, and start choosing.

§7 Good questions

Does my friend need Flipside?

No. A tape link opens in their browser. With their own Apple Music subscription they hear every song in full; without it, a 30-second preview of each. To keep it, they can add Flipside to their Home Screen and paste the link into the rack.

What happens to my tapes on a new phone?

Settings › Your Music › Back Up My Tapes saves one file with every tape, label and note. Open it on the new phone. Add the same songs and the tapes find them.

Does it cost anything?

No. No subscription, no ads, no catch.

Something not working?

The guide has fixes for the usual things. Still stuck? Write to hello@ownyourmedia.club and say which phone you use.
