This is Flipside.

Make someone a mixtape. Pick a C60 or a C90, fill Side A and Side B with songs you love, write the label by hand, and send it to a friend.

Millions of songs are everywhere. Ninety minutes makes you choose, and that’s the whole point.

How it works

Make it. Play it. Send it.

Make it

Press REC, name the tape and pick its length. Every song shows how long it runs, and songs too long for the room left are crossed out. When a side is full, it’s full.

Play it

A cassette deck with real buttons. PLAY/PAUSE stays down while it plays, REW and FF wind with the sound of the tape, and FLIP turns it over. No shuffle, no skipping.

Send it

Send Mixtape lists the people you share your Plex server with. They get a link that opens your tape, with its label and who it’s from, and hear it from your music.

Make it yours

A tape that looks like you made it.

Label Maker

Write the label

Lined, kraft, graph paper, retro stripes or midnight. Marker, neat print, block caps or label-maker tape, in the ink you pick. Long titles shrink and wrap so they always fit. Stuck? Press Surprise Me.

Search any song

Not just what you own

Search for any song by title and artist, not just your library. If it’s already in your Plex library, it fills right in. If it isn’t yet, it holds its place on the tape with links to buy it.

Deck colors

Two sides, one player

Night Black, Metallic Blue, Pearl & Teal, or the rugged Sport line in seven colors. Turn the player over and the menu side matches, screen and all. On a computer, Desktop Mode wears it too.

Songs you don’t own yet

Save its place. Buy it. Watch it fill in.

Put a song on a tape before you own it. It holds its spot and its time, with links to buy it on Bandcamp, Qobuz or the iTunes Store. Add the file to your Plex music and it fills in on the tape by itself.

Powered by myStereo

Your music stays yours.

Flipside plays from your own Plex server. Tapes save to Plex, so they follow you to every device and show up in myStereo too. No Plex yet? Try it with the demo music first.

What do I need?

A Plex Media Server with your music, yours or one a friend shares with you. An iPhone (Safari), an Android phone (Chrome) or a computer. Nothing to download from an app store.

How is it different from myStereo?

myStereo does it all: music, videos, games, faceplates. Flipside does one thing, mixtapes, with a deck that’s all buttons. Press Menu to turn it over for your music, tapes and settings.

Can I put it on my home screen?

Yes. iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app). Either way it gets its own cassette icon and opens full screen.

Troubleshooting

Something not working?

First PLAY is silent

Phones only start sound after a tap. Press PLAY again, and check the volume and the iPhone ring switch.

Can’t reach Plex

Make sure your server is on with Remote Access turned on, then go to Settings › Music Sources › Plex › Reconnect.

My friend can’t play my tape

They hear it from your server, so share your Plex library with them first, then send the tape again.

A tape is missing on my other phone

Choose Mixtapes › Sync with Plex on both devices. They need to be on the same Plex server.

Android Back button

Back steps out of menus like the MENU key. On the deck with nothing open, it leaves the app.

The icon looks old

Remove Flipside from your Home Screen and add it again. Your sign-in and tapes stay.
