A mixtape is a few songs picked for one person, in an order that means something. Flipside gives you a C60 or a C90, two sides, a label you write yourself and a cassette deck with real keys. Ninety minutes makes you choose. That’s the whole point.

Liner notes

I love film scores, especially from the 1990s, like Jurassic Park or Casper. The teenage me used a Yorx stereo system to build “greatest hits” across different film genres, or from specific film composers. From the “Best of John Williams” to “Unexpected Movie Cues”, I took the melodies from these film stories, and wove stories of my own. Transforming one beauty into another (or at least I like to think so).

Playlists look a lot like mixtapes, but they really are not. The mixtape requires thought, requires focus, and most of all requires a story and a time limit. Flipside feels like a return to the best times I had with music, and the first mixtapes I made on it were film scores!

On the bench

Every tape gets a label you make yourself. Tap Surprise Me, pick a theme, or choose each part: the design, paper, pen, ink and shell. Liner notes go with the tape when you send it.

Deck colors too: Night Black, Metallic Blue, Pearl & Teal and more. Turn the player over and the menu side matches.

Real Tape

Lift a tape in the rack and press TAPE OUT. Flipside checks that each side fits, with the quiet between songs counted, and walks you through a short list: phone wired to the deck’s line in, Do Not Disturb on, volume up, blank tape at the start of Side A.

Set your levels with a tone, press record, and Flipside plays the side with four seconds of quiet between songs, so your deck finds every break. Every Flipside C90 fits a real C90.

Bonus tracksA Want List: found the perfect song but don’t own it yet? Save its spot on the tape, with links to buy it from Bandcamp, Qobuz or the iTunes Store. It won’t play until it’s yours; add the file and it fills itself in. Songs on your phone play with no signal. And Flipside is the Tapes button on myStereo: same tapes, same deck.

Get it on your phone

  1. Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: open flipside.ownyourmedia.club in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

  2. Tell it where your music is

    This Phone picks songs from the Files app. My Plex brings your whole library. Just looking? Try the demo songs.

  3. Press REC

    Name it, pick C60 or C90, and start choosing.

Questions

Can I put songs I don’t own on a tape?

Not to play. The Want List saves a spot for a song you plan to buy, with links to buy it. Until the file is in your library, it won’t play, it won’t record to a real tape and it’s left off Apple Music and Spotify copies. Tapes are built from music you own.

Does my friend need Flipside?

No. A tape link opens in their browser. With their own Apple Music they hear every song in full; without it, a 30-second preview of each. To keep it, they can add Flipside to their Home Screen and paste the link into the rack.

What happens to my tapes on a new phone?

Settings › Your Music › Back Up My Tapes saves one file with every tape, label and note. Open it on the new phone. Add the same songs and the tapes find them.

Something not working?

The guide has fixes. Still stuck? Write to hello@ownyourmedia.club and say which phone you use.
