Fair questions

Do I need to join to use myStereo?

No. myStereo, myStereo Elite and myShelf stay free for everyone. Membership is for people who want the card and the perks.

Will it renew on its own?

Card only never renews. Member and Backstage renew through Substack, and you can cancel there anytime.

New to owning music?

Read Start here. It’s easier than you think, and you don’t have to quit streaming.

Where does the money go?

Printing cards, stickers, postage, keeping the sites running, and the occasional record.

Which shops?

We’re talking with Twin Cities places we already love and send people to. We’ll list them here when it’s official.
