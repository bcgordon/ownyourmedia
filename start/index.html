Why own it?

Four plain reasons

  • It staysStreaming libraries change when licenses do. Albums and songs disappear. What you own stays put.
  • You chooseNo algorithm deciding what’s next. You pick an album and listen to the whole thing.
  • Artists get moreBuying an album pays an artist far more than streaming it does.
  • No ads, no changesNobody can put ads in it, change the price or move your music around.

Pick your starting point

Start small. Go further only if you want to.

Level 1 · Starter

Ten albums on your phone

You need: your phone. That’s it.

  1. Pick the ten albums you’d be saddest to lose.
  2. Buy them as downloads, or rip CDs you already have.
  3. Save them to your phone’s Files.
  4. Open myStereo: On Phone › Add Music.
Level 2 · Collector

A library on your computer

You need: a computer and a few spare evenings.

  1. Keep all your music in one folder on your computer.
  2. Add to it as you go: used CDs, downloads, records with download codes.
  3. Copy your favorites to your phone for the road.
Level 3 · Home server

Your music, everywhere

You need: an always-on computer or a small server.

  1. Install Plex, a free app, and point it at your music folder.
  2. Sign in to Plex inside myStereo.
  3. Play your whole library on your phone, at home or away, like streaming but yours.

Where to get music

Cheaper than you’d guess

  • Your own shelvesOld CDs in a closet or basement are free music. Rip them once and keep them forever.
  • Used CDsRecord stores and thrift shops sell them for a few dollars each.
  • DownloadsMany artists sell albums as downloads directly, and many records come with a download code.

How to rip CDs

One evening, then it’s yours for good

What you need

A computer and a CD drive

Most new laptops don’t have a CD drive. A USB one costs about $25 and works on any Mac or PC. Rip the CDs you own, for your own listening.

Best format

Rip once, in full quality

  • FLACThe best choice. Exactly as good as the CD, about half the size, and Plex and myStereo play it. About 300 MB per album
  • Apple Lossless (ALAC)Same quality as FLAC. Pick it if you mostly use Apple’s Music app. About 300 MB per album
  • MP3 320 or AAC 256Only if you’re short on space. You can’t get the lost quality back later. About 100 MB per album

Free tools that do it well

  • WindowsExact Audio CopyThe most careful ripper. It rereads scratched spots and checks your copy against other people’s rips (AccurateRip). A little old-fashioned to set up.
  • MacXLD (X Lossless Decoder)The Mac favorite. Careful like EAC, and simple once it’s set to FLAC.
  • Windows · Mac · Linuxfre:acThe easiest to start with. Pick FLAC, insert a CD, press rip.
  • Already on your computerMedia Player or Apple MusicWindows Media Player can rip to FLAC, and Apple Music can rip to Apple Lossless. Change the format in settings first; both start with small, lower-quality files.
  • Fix names and coversMusicBrainz PicardFree on every computer. It looks up each album online and fills in song names, years and cover art.
  • Online, freeMusicBrainz and AccurateRipThe free databases behind the tools above. They supply album info and confirm your rip is perfect. No sign-up needed.
Step by step

Your first CD

  1. Install one of the free tools above.
  2. In its settings, choose FLAC, and turn on AccurateRip if it offers it.
  3. Choose one music folder on your computer for everything.
  4. Put in a CD. Check that the album and song names came up right.
  5. Press rip. A careful rip takes about 5 to 15 minutes.
  6. Make sure the album has a cover. If not, MusicBrainz Picard can add one.
  7. Back up the music folder to an external drive now and then.
Keep it tidy

A folder Plex will love

Name folders like this and Plex and myStereo sort everything on their own:

Music /
  Night Ferry /
    Harbor Lights (2024) /
      01 Undertow.flac
      cover.jpg

Most rippers can name files this way for you. Look for “file naming” in their settings.

Troubleshooting

Tap a question

Ripping
Album and song names came up blank

The tool couldn’t find the CD online. Rip it anyway, then open the folder in MusicBrainz Picard to fill in the names. Or type them in before you rip.

It’s very slow, or stops on one song

That’s usually a scratch. Careful rippers reread the bad spot many times. Wipe the CD from the center out with a soft cloth and try again. If one song still won’t finish, let it go and move on. You’ll rarely hear the difference.

AccurateRip says it doesn’t match

Your copy differs from other people’s. Clean the disc and rip again, or try another CD drive. If the CD isn’t in AccurateRip at all, that’s fine. It just hasn’t been checked by anyone yet.

There’s no cover art

Save a picture of the cover as cover.jpg in the album’s folder, or let MusicBrainz Picard add it.

My Mac won’t open XLD

Mac blocks apps that don’t come from the App Store the first time. Right-click XLD, choose Open, then Open again.

One album shows up as several in Plex

The songs have different “album artist” names, often on albums with guest singers. Use MusicBrainz Picard to give every song on the album the same album artist, then refresh the library in Plex.

Downloading
My download is a .zip file on my iPhone

Open the Files app, find it in Downloads, and tap it once. It unzips into a folder. Then add the songs in myStereo under On Phone › Add Music.

I can’t find what I downloaded

On iPhone, look in Files › Downloads. On a computer, look in the Downloads folder.

Which format should I pick when I buy?

FLAC or ALAC if they offer it. MP3 320 is fine if they don’t.

Save to Phone stopped partway

Saving only works while myStereo is open on screen. Open it again and it picks up where it left off. If a song was skipped, choose Save to Phone again and it fills in the gaps.

It says my phone is out of room

Go to Settings › On Phone. Switch Quality to Space Saver, or erase an album you’re done with.

A video won’t save to my phone (Elite)

Only MP4 and MOV videos can be saved. Other videos still stream from Plex when you have a signal.

myStereo
The song is playing, but I hear nothing

This happens when Tone is set to anything but Flat and another app, a call or Siri took over the sound. Press play again. If it’s still quiet, set Settings › Sound › Tone to Flat, then close myStereo fully and reopen it.

The first song won’t start

Press play once more. iPhones sometimes ignore the first play while a speaker or AirPods connect.

It won’t connect to Plex

Make sure your Plex server is on and Remote Access is turned on in Plex. Then go to Settings › Music Sources › Plex › Reconnect.

I don’t see the latest changes

Close myStereo fully (swipe it away) and open it again. Sometimes it takes two tries.

The music stops when my screen locks

Set Settings › Sound › Tone to Flat and reopen the app. Tone presets can stop music when the screen locks.

My mixtape won’t play a song I tap

If you see a red ● REC, the mixtape is still recording, so songs you tap are added to it. Choose Recording on the main menu, then Stop Recording.

Still stuck?

In myStereo, go to Alpha › Send Feedback. It goes straight to BC.
