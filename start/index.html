Why own it?

Four plain reasons

  • It staysStreaming libraries change when licenses do. Albums and songs disappear. What you own stays put.
  • You chooseNo algorithm deciding what’s next. You pick an album and listen to the whole thing.
  • Artists get moreBuying an album pays an artist far more than streaming it does.
  • No ads, no changesNobody can put ads in it, change the price or move your music around.

Pick your starting point

Start small. Go further only if you want to.

Level 1 · Starter

Ten albums on your phone

You need: your phone. That’s it.

  1. Pick the ten albums you’d be saddest to lose.
  2. Buy them as downloads, or rip CDs you already have.
  3. Save them to your phone’s Files.
  4. Open myStereo: Music › On This Phone › Add Music.
Level 2 · Collector

A library on your computer

You need: a computer and a few spare evenings.

  1. Keep all your music in one folder on your computer.
  2. Add to it as you go: used CDs, downloads, records with download codes.
  3. Copy your favorites to your phone for the road.
Level 3 · Home server

Your music, everywhere

You need: an always-on computer or a small server.

  1. Install Plex, a free app, and point it at your music folder.
  2. Sign in to Plex inside myStereo.
  3. Play your whole library on your phone, at home or away, like streaming but yours.

Where to get music

Cheaper than you’d guess

  • Your own shelvesOld CDs in a closet or basement are free music. Rip them once and keep them forever.
  • Used CDsRecord stores and thrift shops sell them for a few dollars each.
  • DownloadsMany artists sell albums as downloads directly, and many records come with a download code.
