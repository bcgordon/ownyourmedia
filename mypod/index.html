Set up in a minute

  1. 1 Add it to your home screen iPhone: open myPod in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. It opens full screen like a real app.
  2. 2 Sign in with Plex Go to Settings › Plex and sign in. myPod finds your server and loads your artists, albums and playlists.
  3. 3 Spin the wheel Drag your thumb in a circle to scroll, press the center to choose. You remember how this goes.

What's inside

Try on a case

Classic white or black, a brown Zune throwback, and a couple of special editions with stickers on the back. Swipe the case to flip it over.

Ratings that stay yours

Give songs one to five stars and build playlists from them. Ratings live on your phone, with a backup file you can keep.

Games in Extras

Brick, Stack, Minesweeper and a Music Quiz that plays clips from your own library.

Speakers welcome

Play through Bluetooth speakers and headphones on any phone. On iPhone, you can also pick AirPlay speakers right from Now Playing.

Browse your way

Artists, albums A to Z, genres, playlists and search. Shuffle a whole genre with one click.

Easy on the eyes

Large text, light and dark screens, and your pick of wheel colors.

Not on the menu

There's a secret in here.

Anyone who grew up with a gray controller in their hands has a head start. Try the wheel on the main menu.

What you need

  • A Plex Media Server with your music on it
  • A free Plex account
  • An iPhone (Safari) or Android phone (Chrome). Computers work too.

Good questions

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads.

Where does my stuff go?

Music streams from your own Plex server. Your settings and ratings stay on your phone.

Is this made by Apple or Plex?

No. It's a fan project, not affiliated with either.
