Before you begin

Own your media.

The songs and films on myPod are yours. Ripped from the CDs and DVDs on your shelf, bought straight from the artists and studios you love, kept on your own server at the quality you chose.

  • Yours to keep.No subscription to cancel, no license that can expire, no album that disappears overnight.

Chapter 1

Getting Started

Put myPod on your Home Screen, sign in to your own Plex server, and your collection is in your pocket.

Add myPod to your Home Screen

  1. Open myPod’s web address in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open myPod from its new icon. It runs full screen, just like an app.

Sign in to Plex

  1. Go to Settings › Plex › Sign In with Plex.
  2. Tap Open Plex sign-in and sign in on the Plex page.
  3. Come back to myPod. It finds your server on its own.
Just looking? Until you sign in, myPod plays a small demo library with no sound, so you can try everything first.

No Plex? Use music on this phone

  1. Go to Music › On This Phone › Add Music. (Not signed in? It’s right on the sign-in screen as Music on This Phone.)
  2. Pick songs from the Files app. You can choose many at once. On a computer or Android phone, Add a Folder brings in a whole folder.
  3. myPod reads each song’s name, artist, album and cover art, and keeps its own copy, so it plays even with no internet.
Good to know: MP3, M4A, AAC, ALAC, FLAC and WAV all work. Songs you add live only on that device. Remove them under On This Phone › Manage.

Chapter 4

Now Playing

Press the center button to change what the wheel does. A small pop-up at the bottom of the screen shows where you are.

1Normal
2Scrub
3Rating
4Shuffle
5Repeat
Scrub
Turn the wheel to move through the song.
Rating
Turn the wheel or tap a star to give the song 1 to 5 stars. Turn all the way down to clear it. The stars then show under the album name.
Shuffle
Off, or Songs. Starts with the song you picked, then mixes the rest.
Repeat
Off, Track (this song again), or Album (the whole list again).

You can also tap a dot in the pop-up, or a choice right on the screen. After a few seconds myPod goes back to Normal on its own.

Volume lives on your iPhone’s side buttons.

Ratings and rated playlists

Music › Ratings turns your stars into playlists that update themselves: My Top Rated (4 and 5 stars), one list for each star count, and Recently Rated. Each list starts with Shuffle, and they play on Plexamp too.

Your ratings are yours alone. They live on this myPod, not in Plex, so nobody else on your server sees them.

Your stars follow you. On a Plex server you own, myPod saves ratings in Plex itself, so they match on your phone, your computer, myPod Elite and Plexamp, and survive a deleted app. Songs saved on this phone, songs on a network share, and songs on a server someone shares with you keep their ratings on this device only. For those, go to Settings › Ratings Backup › Back Up Ratings and save the file to Files. Restore from Backup brings them back.

Chapter 5

Cover Flow

Turn your iPhone sideways and your albums fill the screen.

  1. Swipe left or right to flip through albums. Flick to fly.
  2. Tap the middle album to turn it over and see its songs.
  3. Tap a song to play it. Turn the phone upright, or tap Done, to go back.
Nothing happens when you turn? Swipe down from the top-right corner and turn off Portrait Orientation Lock. You can also open Cover Flow from Music › Cover Flow.

Chapter 7

Play On Another Speaker

Use myPod as a remote for Plexamp anywhere in the house.

  1. Make sure Plexamp is open on the other device.
  2. Go to Play On and choose it. A checkmark shows your pick.
  3. Everything you play now plays there. Choose This myPod to play on your phone again.

AirPlay and Bluetooth

Choose Play On › AirPlay & Bluetooth and your iPhone’s own speaker list pops up: HomePods, Apple TVs, AirPlay speakers, Bluetooth headphones and car stereos. Pick one and myPod keeps playing there. It works for videos too.

Plexamp or AirPlay? Plexamp sends the music from your server straight to that device, so your phone is only the remote. AirPlay and Bluetooth stream from your phone, so keep myPod open nearby.

Chapter 8

Extras

Games

Choose Extras › Games. There are four, and myPod remembers your best score in each.

Brick. The classic. Turn the wheel or drag on the screen to move the paddle. Press the center to launch the ball.

Music Quiz. myPod plays a few seconds from a random song in your library. Pick its name from four choices. The faster you answer, the more points you get. Ten songs a round. It plays on your phone, so it needs your Plex library, not the demo.

Stack. Falling blocks. Turn the wheel or drag sideways to move the block. Press the center or tap the screen to turn it. Press ⏭ or swipe down to drop it. Press ⏯ to pause.

Minesweeper. Open every square that isn’t a mine. A number tells you how many mines touch that square. Tap to open, press and hold to plant a flag. Prefer the wheel? Turn it to move the box, use ⏮ and ⏭ to jump a row, press the center to open, and ⏯ to flag.

While you play Stack or Minesweeper, the ⏮ ⏭ ⏯ buttons control the game instead of the music. Press Menu to leave, and they work on music again.

Flip it over

Swipe sideways on the empty part of the case, like the space around the wheel, and myPod turns over to show its polished steel back, a few stickers, and an engravingdark gunmetal back, engraved myPod Elite · Musaka Edition. Swipe again to flip it back. The music keeps playing.

Sleep Timer

Choose 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, or End of Song. A small moon appears in the top bar while it’s set.

Falling asleep to Plexamp in another room? The timer works most reliably when music is playing on your phone itself.

Chapter 9

Settings

Desktop Mode

On a computer, choose Switch to Desktop Mode under myPod, or Settings › Desktop Mode. myPod becomes a jukebox window made for a mouse: play, pause and skip buttons at the top, a display that shows the song and lets you click the bar to jump ahead, a volume slider, and a Library list down the side. Click any row to open it, and scroll with your mouse. The arrow at the top of the Library list hides it for a wider view. The Mini Player button (or the yellow button in the corner) shrinks myPod to just the controls, the song display and volume. In Chrome and Edge it floats in its own small window above everything else. Click the red button in the corner to go back to the iPod. Desktop Mode needs a large window, so it never shows on a phone.

Chapter 10

myPod Elite

Want extras like playing music straight from your own network share? myPod Elite is for you. It’s the same myPod with features that take a little setup, for people who like to tinker.

Network Share
Play music from your own WebDAV server, with no Plex needed. Browse its folders under Music › Network Share.
Starship looks
Elite opens in the Away Mission case with the Starship Console screen. (Both are in regular myPod’s Settings too.)
Musaka back
Flip it over: a dark gunmetal back engraved myPod Elite · Musaka Edition.
Its own space
Elite keeps its own settings, Plex sign-in, ratings and music, so you can keep both on your Home Screen.

Get myPod Elite

  1. Open ownyourmedia.club/elite in Safari on your iPhone (or Chrome on Android).
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It gets its own myPod Elite icon.

Set up a network share

  1. Your share needs an address that starts with https://. Tailscale, a Cloudflare tunnel or a reverse proxy can give it one.
  2. The server has to let myPod in. The easiest way is a recent version of rclone:
    rclone serve webdav /path/to/Music --user NAME --pass SECRET --allow-origin https://ownyourmedia.club
  3. In Elite, go to Settings › Network Share › Connect a Share and enter the address, username and password.
  4. Open Music › Network Share, pick a folder, and press Play All or Shuffle.
Good to know: each song downloads fully before it plays, which is quick on a home network. Folders laid out as Artist › Album, with a cover.jpg in each album folder, look best. Elite’s Network Share › Setup Help has the details for other servers.

Chapter 11

Tips and Troubleshooting

Can’t reach Plex
Make sure your server is on and Remote Access is turned on in Plex. Then try Settings › Plex › Reconnect.
Song won’t play
myPod tries a second, converted stream automatically. If it still won’t play, the file may be damaged on your server.
Plexamp ignores myPod
Open Plexamp on that device, then pick it again in Play On.
Icon looks old
Delete myPod from your Home Screen and add it again from Safari. Your sign-in and settings stay.
Start fresh
Settings › Plex › Sign Out, then sign in again.
