Club rules

  1. Own it.

    Buy the records, films and books you love, and keep them. Nobody can take back what you own.

  2. Play it.

    Actually use what’s on your shelf. Put on a whole album, start to finish, or build the ultimate party mixtape.

  3. Share it.

    Bring it to the table: a listening night, a swap, a mixtape made for one person. It’s all better with your people.
