A free stereo for the music you own

Your music.
Your stereo.

myStereo turns your phone into a stereo for the albums and movies you already own. It plays songs or movies stored right on your phone. You can also connect a Plex server, but it’s not required. No subscription, no ads, no algorithm. You pick the album, turn the knob and press play.

Free · iPhone, Android and computer · Try it with the demo music first

§1 The inputs

Three options under the screen, like the inputs on an old receiver. Plus simple access to your videos, easy setup tools and fun extras.

Music

Your whole library: artists, albums, genres, playlists, ratings and search. Spin the knob through it, or just touch the screen.

Tapes

Our mixtape tool, Flipside, lives right inside myStereo. Press TAPES and the deck comes up with every mixtape you’ve made.

Vinyl

The turntable. Pick any side, A through D, and press the center of the knob to turn the sleeve over: the songs, the pressing and how many times you’ve spun it. Every CD is now a record.

Video

Your movies and shows, right on the display. Turn the phone sideways for full screen. It picks up where you left off. (Plex server optional.)

§2 On your desk

The walnut console

Open myStereo on a computer and it becomes a walnut console. The OYM turntable sits on the left and the cassette deck on the right. Click either one.

A wall of covers

Albums show as a wall of covers, and movies and shows as a wall of posters, with a red line under anything you’ve started.

Now Playing

A column of clickable songs. With a record on, it shows the sides and the color of the pressing.

The Mini Player

One small walnut bar with the cover, the song, the keys and the volume. In Chrome and Edge it floats in its own window.

§3 Make it yours

Faceplates

Black, white, walnut and a dozen colors, plus special editions like Runaway Anchor and Field Scanner.

Displays

Pale green LCD like an old car stereo, amber, teal, light or dark. Large text is on from the start.

Tone

Presets for the phone speaker, earbuds, the car or quiet listening. Or set the bass and treble yourself.

The back

Swipe the case and it turns over: polished steel, a few stickers and the engraving. The music keeps playing.

§4 No signal, no problem

Save to Phone

Save albums and playlists before a flight, a road trip or a weekend up north. They play with no signal and use none of your data.

No Plex?

Add songs you’ve ripped or bought from the Files app. myStereo keeps its own copy, so they play anywhere.

The source light

A little light on the screen says where the music is coming from: PLEX or PHONE. Use both? Tap it to switch.

Keep your 5-stars

Turn it on and every song you rate five stars saves itself to your phone.

§5 Extras

Cover Flow

Turn your phone sideways and your albums fill the screen. Flick through them, tap one to turn it over.

Games

Brick, Snake, Minesweeper, and a Music Quiz that plays clips from your own library.

Play it somewhere else

Send it to Plexamp in another room, or to AirPlay speakers, a HomePod or Bluetooth.

A secret or two

Remember the code that gave you 30 lives in Contra? Try it on the main menu. The edges of the knob are the D-pad.

§6 For tinkerers

Have your music on your own server, not in Plex? myStereo can play straight from a network share (WebDAV). It takes a little setup, and the guide walks you through it.

§7 Set up in a minute

  1. Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: open mystereo.ownyourmedia.club in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen. It opens full screen, like a real app.

  2. Add your own songs, or sign in to Plex

    Tap the source light to PHONE and add songs from the Files app. Have Plex? Settings › Music Sources › Plex.

  3. Turn the knob

    Drag your thumb in a circle to scroll, press the center to choose. You remember how this goes.

§8 Good questions

Does it cost anything?

No. No subscription, no ads, no catch.

What do I need?

An iPhone (Safari), an Android phone (Chrome) or a computer, and some songs you own. A Plex server is optional: connect yours, or one a friend shares with you, for your whole library. Want one? Here’s how to build one.

Where does my stuff go?

Songs and movies you add live on your phone. With Plex, they stream from your own server. Your settings stay on your phone.

Is this made by Apple or Plex?

No. It’s a fan project, not affiliated with either.
