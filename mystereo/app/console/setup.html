setting up console.

About ten minutes, once. iPhone menus move around between iOS versions, so the paths below are close, not exact.

1. put it on the home screen

  1. In Safari, open mystereo.ownyourmedia.club/mystereo/app/console/
  2. Share › Add to Home Screen. Keep the name Console.
  3. From now on, open it from that icon. That's what makes it full screen, with no Safari bars.

2. bring the stereo in

  1. Tap the strip under the clock. myStereo slides up.
  2. Sign in to Plex there, once. An iPhone keeps each Home Screen app separate, so Console's myStereo is a fresh sign-in.
  3. Your tapes are behind myStereo's TAPES key, the same as always. Anything missing: Paste a Tape Link, or send it to yourself.
  4. ‹ console. at the top takes you home. The music keeps playing, and the strip shows what's on.

3. make your six buttons

In Console, tap set. Under buttons, give each of the six a name and say what it opens.

  1. An app: works for any app, through Shortcuts. In the Shortcuts app: + › Add Action › Open App › pick the app. Name the shortcut exactly what Console shows under the Shortcut's name (it suggests one, like Console Phone).
  2. A link: some apps open straight from a link, with no Shortcut. Tap one of the picks (Signal, Podcasts, Music, Maps…) or type the app's link.
  3. A Shortcut flashes on screen for a moment before the app opens. That's normal.
  4. If a link doesn't open its app, switch that button to an app and make it a Shortcut. Phone, Messages (to land on your conversations) and most other apps need one.

Put them back, under set, returns the six to how Console came.

4. a focus with one icon

  1. Settings › Focus › + › Custom. Call it Console.
  2. People: let your favorites through. Apps: the ones you talk to people with, like Phone and Messages.
  3. Customize Screens › Home Screen: make a page with only the Console icon on it and pick just that page.
  4. Turn the Focus on. Swiping up lands on that one page, one tap from Console.

5. take the color out (optional)

  1. Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Color Filters › Grayscale.
  2. Settings › Accessibility › Accessibility Shortcut › Color Filters, so a triple-click of the side button turns it on and off.

6. last bits

  1. In Console, tap set and find your town for the weather.
  2. Pick light or dark with the button up top. It stays where you left it.
  3. Under colors, pick the background for light and for dark: the Typewriter look, a swatch, or any color with +.
  4. Under quote, write a line for the bottom of the home screen: a quote, a reminder. Tapping that line takes you there too.

Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility) can lock the phone into Console if you want to go all the way.
