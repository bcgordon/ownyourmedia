setting up console.

About ten minutes, once, on an iPhone or an Android phone. Phone menus move around between versions, so the paths below are close, not exact.

1. put it on the home screen

iPhone

  1. In Safari, open mystereo.ownyourmedia.club/mystereo/app/console/
  2. Share › Add to Home Screen. Keep the name Console.

Android

  1. In Chrome, open mystereo.ownyourmedia.club/mystereo/app/console/
  2. The ⋮ menu › Add to Home screen (or Install app). Keep the name Console.

From now on, open it from that icon. That's what makes it full screen, with no browser bars.

2. bring the stereo in

  1. Tap the myStereo key under the clock. myStereo slides up.
  2. Sign in to Plex there, once. On an iPhone each Home Screen app is kept separate, so Console's myStereo is a fresh sign-in.
  3. Your tapes are behind myStereo's TAPES key, the same as always. Anything missing: Paste a Tape Link, or send it to yourself.
  4. ‹ console. at the top takes you home. The music keeps playing, and the key shows what's on.

3. make your six buttons

In Console, tap the gear (top left) for settings. Under buttons, give each of the six a name and say what it opens.

iPhone

  1. An app: works for any app, through Shortcuts. In the Shortcuts app: + › Add Action › Open App › pick the app. Name the shortcut exactly what Console shows under the Shortcut's name (it suggests one, like Console Phone).
  2. A link: some apps open straight from a link, with no Shortcut. Tap one of the picks (Signal, Podcasts, Music, Maps…) or type the app's link.
  3. A Shortcut flashes on screen for a moment before the app opens. That's normal.
  4. If a link doesn't open its app, switch that button to an app and make it a Shortcut. Phone, Messages (to land on your conversations) and most other apps need one.

Android

  1. An app: tap one of the picks (Signal, WhatsApp, Spotify, Maps…), or type the app's Android name. It's on the app's Google Play page, after id= in the address, like com.spotify.music.
  2. A link: Phone opens the dialer, Texts your messages app, Email your mail app.
  3. If an app isn't on the phone, its button opens it on Google Play instead.

Put them back, in settings, returns the six to how Console came.

4. one icon, fewer pulls

iPhone

  1. Settings › Focus › + › Custom. Call it Console.
  2. People: let your favorites through. Apps: the ones you talk to people with, like Phone and Messages.
  3. Customize Screens › Home Screen: make a page with only the Console icon on it and pick just that page.
  4. Turn the Focus on. Swiping up lands on that one page, one tap from Console.

Android

  1. Settings › Digital Wellbeing › Focus mode: pick the apps that pull at you. They pause while it's on.
  2. Put Console on your first home screen page by itself, and the rest of your apps in a folder or the app drawer.

5. take the color out (optional)

iPhone

  1. Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Color Filters › Grayscale.
  2. Settings › Accessibility › Accessibility Shortcut › Color Filters, so a triple-click of the side button turns it on and off.

Android

  1. Settings › Accessibility › Color correction › Grayscale. On some phones it's under Digital Wellbeing › Bedtime mode › Grayscale.

6. last bits

  1. In Console, tap the gear and find your town for the weather.
  2. Pick light or dark at the top of settings. It stays where you left it.
  3. Under colors, pick the background for light and for dark: the Typewriter look, a swatch, or any color with +.
  4. Under quote, write a line for the bottom of the home screen: a quote, a reminder. Tapping that line takes you there too.

To lock the phone into Console if you want to go all the way: Guided Access on an iPhone (Settings › Accessibility), App pinning on Android (Settings › Security).
