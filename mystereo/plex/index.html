Plex is free software that turns a computer at home into your own private music service. Set it up once, and your albums play anywhere in myStereo and Flipside. Plan on an afternoon, plus however long it takes to rip your CDs.

Not ready for a server? myStereo and Flipside also play songs saved right on your phone.

Know someone with Plex? Server owners can share their music library with your free Plex account. It’s the fastest way to try myStereo.

Seven steps

  1. Pick a home for your music

    Plex runs on a computer at home that stays on and plugged in. An old laptop or desktop works fine, and so does a Mac mini or a network drive (NAS). Windows, Mac and Linux all work. How much space? About 300 MB an album in lossless, or about 100 MB as MP3. A 1 TB drive holds thousands of albums.

  2. Install Plex Media Server

    Make a free account at plex.tv, then download Plex Media Server from the Plex downloads page. Install it and sign in. It runs quietly in the background.

  3. Fill it with music you own

    Put all your music in one folder. Rip your CDs (lossless ALAC or FLAC sounds best; XLD on a Mac and Exact Audio Copy on Windows are free). Buy downloads you keep, from Bandcamp or the download code in a new record’s sleeve. Digitize records and tapes with a USB turntable or deck and Audacity. Old iTunes folders and MP3s count too.

  4. Tidy it up

    Plex works best with a folder for each album: Artist › Album › songs. The free app MusicBrainz Picard fixes names and adds album art.

  5. Add a Music library in Plex

    In Plex, go to Settings › Libraries › Add Library, choose Music, and point it at your music folder. Plex scans everything and adds the artwork and album details itself.

  6. Listen away from home (optional)

    Turn on Remote Access in your Plex server settings. Plex charges for streaming video away from home, but music isn’t part of that rule right now. Plex changes its plans from time to time, so check their remote playback page.

  7. Sign in from myStereo

    In myStereo, go to Settings › Music Sources › Plex and sign in. It finds your server and loads your artists, albums and playlists. Flipside uses the same sign-in.
